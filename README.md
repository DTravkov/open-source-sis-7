## A short explanation of GPL license. Repo exists only as example for pull requests, has nothin to document, so take this.

The GNU General Public License (GPL) is a widely used copyleft license created to ensure that computer software remains permanently free and open-source. At its core, the GPL grants users the legal right to run, study, modify, and distribute the software without financial cost.

However, the defining feature of the GPL—often referred to as its "viral" mechanism—is its strict reciprocity requirement. If you take GPL-licensed code, integrate it into your own project, and distribute that public product, you are legally obligated to release your entire source code under the exact same GPL terms.

This creates a powerful legal safeguard for the open-source community. It prevents corporate entities or proprietary developers from taking freely shared code, locking the source code behind closed doors, and selling it as a commercial product without contributing back. Ultimately, the GPL acts as a legal instrument that uses copyright law to protect and perpetuate software freedom, ensuring that improvements made by anyone remain accessible to everyone.
